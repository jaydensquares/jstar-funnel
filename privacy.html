Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

JStar Acquisitions ("we," "us") buys residential real estate. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, phone number, email address, and details about your property and situation (address, condition, timeline, asking price, occupancy, mortgage status, and anything you type in the form).
  • Information collected automatically: your IP address, browser type, the page you came from, and advertising click identifiers (such as UTM tags, Google and Meta click IDs). We use cookies and similar tools from Meta, Google, and our CRM provider to measure ads and record form submissions.
  • Public property data: we look up public records and market data about the property address you submit (for example square footage, year built, and nearby sales) to prepare an offer.

How we use your information

  • To review your property, prepare a cash offer, and contact you about selling it.
  • To send you calls, texts, or emails about your property, if you agreed to receive them.
  • To measure and improve our advertising and website.
  • To meet legal requirements and protect our rights.

Text messages (SMS)

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

If you check the consent box on our form, we may text you at the number you provided about selling your property. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. See our Terms & Conditions for full messaging terms.

When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us run our business (website hosting, CRM and messaging, property data, email). They may use it only to provide services to us.
  • With parties to your transaction, such as a title company or closing attorney, and, if we assign our purchase contract, the buyer who takes our place, limited to what is needed to complete the sale.
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

The SMS restrictions above apply in every case: your phone number and text-messaging consent are never shared for anyone else's marketing.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop messages.
  • Ask us to stop calling or emailing you, or to delete your information, by contacting us below.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Data retention and security

We keep your information as long as needed to discuss and complete a possible purchase and to meet legal and record-keeping requirements. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

This website is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
