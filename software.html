Questions

Before you sign up

Where do the numbers come from?

Property details and recent sales come from public records and listing data covering the U.S. After-repair value is based on the closest recent sales by distance, size, age and date. Repair estimates use cost per square foot by condition, adjusted for the age of the house and any problems the seller reports. Every number is an estimate, so check comps and walk the property before you sign.

How do offers get calculated?

Max offer = after-repair value × 70%, minus repairs, minus your assignment fee. Your opening offer starts 5% below that so you have room to negotiate. On any deal you can change the ARV, repairs, contract price and fee and see the effect on your buyer's profit.

What doesn't it do?

JStar Pro doesn't place calls, send texts or skip trace phone numbers. Owner lists export as a CSV you can upload to your skip-tracing service. Distress data such as pre-foreclosures and tax liens isn't included yet.

Do I need my own website or domain?

No. Your funnel is live at your own link as soon as you sign up. On Pro and Team, or with the add-on on Starter, you can connect a domain you own. We give you the two DNS records to add at your registrar.

Can I use my own ads?

Yes. Paste your Meta Pixel ID and Google Ads conversion tag in Ad tracking, and point your ads at your funnel link. Each lead shows which ad platform it came from. Add utm_source to your ad links for finer tracking.

Are the contracts legally ready to use?

They are templates with the disclosures Texas requires for wholesalers, filled in from your deal. Laws vary by state and situation. Have a real estate attorney in your state review them once before you use them, and follow your state's rules on marketing contracts you don't own.

What happens if I hit my monthly limit?

Your funnel keeps taking leads. They still arrive in your inbox with the seller's details, just without the automatic analysis, until you upgrade or the month resets. Plan limits reset on the 1st and unused usage doesn't roll over. Anything you purchase separately as an add-on doesn't reset.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, from Plan & account → Manage billing. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
