Disclaimers

JStar Acquisitions is a real estate investment company. We are not licensed real estate agents or brokers, and we do not represent you in the sale of your property.

How our offers work: If you accept our offer, we sign a purchase contract to buy your property. We may close on the purchase ourselves, or we may assign (transfer) our rights under that contract to another buyer before closing. If we assign it, that buyer takes our place and closes on the same price and terms you agreed to. We may be paid an assignment fee by that buyer, which does not come out of your proceeds.

Until closing, we hold only an equitable interest in your property under the contract, not legal title. We will give you this disclosure in writing before you sign any contract, and we follow the wholesaling and disclosure laws of the state where your property is located.

Requesting an offer does not obligate you to sell. You are welcome to talk with a real estate agent or attorney before signing anything.
