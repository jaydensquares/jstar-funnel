Questions

What sellers ask us most

Do I need to make repairs or clean out the house?

No. We buy houses as-is, in any condition. Take what you want and leave the rest.

How do you come up with the offer?

We look at recent sales of similar homes nearby, the repairs the house needs, and your timeline. Then we give you one clear number and walk you through how we got there.

Are there any fees or commissions?

No commissions and no fees to you. We pay standard closing costs.

How fast can you close?

In as little as 7 days if the title is clear, or on whatever date works for you.

Will you be the one buying my house?

We may close on it ourselves, or we may assign our contract to another buyer who closes on the same price and terms you agreed to. We tell you this in writing before you sign anything. The details are in the disclaimers below.

Am I obligated to sell if I ask for an offer?

No. The offer is free and you can say no. You're welcome to talk with an agent or attorney before signing anything.

Disclaimers

JStar Acquisitions is a real estate investment company. We are not licensed real estate agents or brokers, and we do not represent you in the sale of your property.

How our offers work: If you accept our offer, we sign a purchase contract to buy your property. We may close on the purchase ourselves, or we may assign (transfer) our rights under that contract to another buyer before closing. If we assign it, that buyer takes our place and closes on the same price and terms you agreed to. We may be paid an assignment fee by that buyer, which does not come out of your proceeds.

Until closing, we hold only an equitable interest in your property under the contract, not legal title. We will give you this disclosure in writing before you sign any contract, and we follow the wholesaling and disclosure laws of the state where your property is located.

Requesting an offer does not obligate you to sell. You are welcome to talk with a real estate agent or attorney before signing anything.
