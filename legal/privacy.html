Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

This policy explains what JStar Pro ("we," "us") collects when you use getjstarpro.com and the JStar Pro software, how we use it and the choices you have. It covers two groups of people: customers (the investors and wholesalers who sign up) and homeowners who fill out a customer's seller funnel.

Short version

  • We collect what's needed to run your account, bill you and run your tools.
  • We don't sell personal information and we don't share one customer's leads with another.
  • We send data to a small set of providers (hosting, payments, email, property data and AI) only to make the features work.
  • You can ask us to see, fix or delete your information through the support page.

Information about customers

  • Account details: name, business name, email, phone, username and a hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).
  • Billing: your plan, subscription status and billing history. Card details are collected and stored by Stripe, not by us.
  • Settings: business address, notification email, custom domain and any ad tracking IDs you enter.
  • What you put in the tools: addresses you analyze, property photos, deals, notes, contracts you generate and lists you pull.
  • Usage: counts of analyses and other actions so we can apply plan limits, plus basic server logs (IP address, browser, pages and errors) kept by our host for security and troubleshooting.

Information about homeowners

When a homeowner fills out a customer's seller funnel, we collect what they enter: property address and details, condition, situation, name, phone, email and their consent to be contacted. We store it for that customer and send them a notification. The customer decides how to follow up, and their own privacy policy applies to that contact. For this information we act on the customer's behalf. Homeowners who want their information removed can ask the customer directly or contact us through the support page and we'll pass the request along and help carry it out.

Property analyses also pull public records about the property, such as estimated value, recorded owner, recorded mortgages and foreclosure filings. That information comes from public records and data providers, not from the homeowner.

How we use information

  • to provide the Service: run analyses, estimate repairs, look up mortgage and equity, generate contracts, host funnels and send lead alerts;
  • to bill you, apply plan limits and send receipts, password resets and account notices;
  • to keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems;
  • to answer support requests;
  • to meet legal obligations.

We don't use your leads or uploads to market to homeowners, and we don't sell or rent personal information. We don't use it for targeted advertising across other companies' sites.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or as part of a merger or sale of the business (and we'll tell you if that happens). If you turn on Meta or Google ad tracking on your funnel, those companies receive event data from your funnel under their own policies.

Cookies

We use one sign-in cookie to keep you logged in and your browser's local storage for small preferences, like whether you've finished the welcome tour. We don't use advertising cookies on getjstarpro.com. Customer funnels may use the ad tracking the customer turns on.

How long we keep it

We keep account and lead data while your account is open. Property lookups are cached for up to 30 days so repeat runs are fast. After you close your account, we delete your data within 90 days, except billing records we must keep for tax and accounting, and backups that roll off on their own schedule.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored in private storage. Passwords are hashed, sign-in links expire, and reset links work only once. No system is perfectly secure, so use a strong password and tell us right away if you see anything off.

Your choices and rights

You can update your account details in Account settings, delete leads and deals in the app, and cancel any time. You can also ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it or delete it. Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Virginia or Texas), you may have more rights under state law, and we'll honor them. We won't treat you differently for making a request. To make one, use the support page; we may need to confirm it's really you.

Children

JStar Pro is for businesses and isn't meant for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we make a material change to this policy, we'll update the date above and email customers before it takes effect.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: use the support page.
