Questions

The short version of the Affiliate Terms.

Do I need to pay for JStar Pro to join?

No. All you need is a free JStar Pro login. Sign up, open the Earn 30% tab and get your link.

How much do I earn?

30% of every subscription payment a customer you send makes, for their first 12 months. On the $149 Pro plan that's $44.70 a month per customer. There's no cap on how many customers you send.

How long does my link track?

60 days. If someone clicks your link and signs up within 60 days, they're yours. If they click another affiliate's link after yours, the last click counts.

When and how do I get paid?

Each commission is held for 30 days in case of a refund, then it's ready. Payouts go out on the 1st and 15th once you have $50 or more, by PayPal, Zelle, Venmo or Cash App.

Can I see my stats?

Yes. Your dashboard shows link clicks, signups, paying customers and every commission, with what's pending, ready and paid.

Do I have to say I'm an affiliate?

Yes. The FTC requires a clear disclosure when you earn a commission, like "#ad" or "I earn a commission if you sign up." No income claims, no spam, and no bidding on "JStar Pro" in search ads.

Do taxes apply?

You're responsible for your own taxes. If you earn $600 or more in a year, we'll ask for a W-9 before paying more.
