Tools

Run an addressOpen

Type any address, whether it's a call-in, a list lead or a house you drove by. In about ten seconds you get recent comparable sales, the after-repair value, a repair estimate by condition, your max and opening offer, the recorded mortgage balance and equity with any liens or pre-foreclosure, and a deal sheet with a ready-to-send buyer pitch. Add exact repair line items to replace the estimate. Works for houses and vacant lots.

Uses 1 deal analysis
Batch analysisOpen

Paste up to 10 addresses, set the condition for each, and get every offer in one table, sorted by score. Tap any row for its full deal sheet or export the table to CSV.

Uses 1 deal analysis per address
Photo repair estimateOpen

Upload up to 12 listing or walkthrough photos. You get a condition grade, line-by-line repair costs with low and high ranges, red flags like water damage or foundation cracks, and what to check on the walkthrough. Send the repair lines straight into Run an address.

Uses 1 photo estimate
Deals and underwritingOpen

Your pipeline from lead to offer, contract, assigned and closed, with pending and closed fees. Each deal has an underwriting calculator (buyer price, buyer profit, 70% rule), a numbers sign-off, an 18-point due-diligence checklist, and a purchase agreement and assignment filled in from the deal.

Ad tracking

Connect your Meta Pixel and Google Ads tag so each funnel lead counts as a conversion.

Meta (Facebook / Instagram)

Where do I find these?
  1. Open Meta Events Manager → Data sources → your Pixel.
  2. The Pixel ID is the number under its name.
  3. For the token: Settings → Conversions API → Generate access token.

Google Ads

Where do I find these?
  1. Google Ads → Goals → Conversions → New conversion action → Website → "Submit lead form".
  2. Choose "Use Google tag" → install manually.
  3. In the event snippet, send_to looks like AW-123456789/AbC-D_efG. The part before the slash is the tag ID; after it is the label.

Your funnel fires your Pixel and Google tag on every visit and sends a Lead conversion when a seller submits. Each lead in your inbox is tagged with where it came from (Google Ads, Meta, or the utm_source on your ad link).
